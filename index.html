Hello.

I am a researcher in the lab of Eric-Jan Wagenmakers at the University of Amsterdam and a former DFG Walter-Benjamin fellow. My research centers on (Bayesian) statistical modeling, in particular, psychometrics, reliability estimation, and structural equation modeling. I develop open-source tools for JASP (BFpack, Factor, Reliability, SEM) and the R package Bayesrel, and I co-founded JASP Services BV. I strongly believe that science should be accessible to everyone. To that end, I strive to publish open-access content whenever possible, and I ensure that the methods I develop are implemented in free and open-source software platforms, such as R and JASP.
