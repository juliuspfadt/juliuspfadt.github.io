I am a researcher in the lab of Eric-Jan Wagenmakers at the University of Amsterdam and a former DFG Walter-Benjamin fellow. My research centers on (Bayesian) statistical modeling, in particular, psychometrics, reliability estimation, and structural equation modeling. I develop open-source tools for [JASP](https://jasp-stats.org) (BFpack, Factor, Reliability, SEM) and the R package Bayesrel, and I co-founded [JASP Services BV](https://jasp-services.com). I am committed to open science and accessible methodology.
